PHASE 6 -- FOOD PATCHES + OPERANT SCHEDULES
-------------------------------------------
[x] Single renewable food patch: logistic regrowth + depletion on contact;
    food intensity/contact scale with biomass (VI-like availability). Max
    regrowth (rK/4) < intake rate, so a camper depletes to the floor then lives
    off the steady regrowth.
[x] Concurrent VI-VI matching via DISCRIMINATIVE CUES (matching.py): two
    cue-marked patches (one cue dimension, not new food channels) on different VI
    schedules; learned cue->rate value (CueReceptorField-style) drives approach;
    travel between patches = COD. GML fit log(B_L/B_R)=a*log(R_L/R_R)+log b gives
    a=0.69 (undermatching, as in real animals), R^2=0.80. exp008_matching.
[x] Sweep COD (patch separation / travel): matching sensitivity a rises with
    COD -- 0.04 (sep2) -> 0.88 (sep14), reproducing Shull & Pliskoff. exp009.
[x] Multi-alternative matching (exp010): N=5 cue-marked patches on a circle,
    alt-vs-rest GML a=0.99, R^2=0.75 -- near-perfect matching, no new machinery.
[x] Canonical counterbalanced matching (exp012): ratios 9:1..1:9 + 1:1,
    cue<->side counterbalanced. Found a spurious SIDE bias from an even-grid
    wall asymmetry; fixed with an odd grid (MatchConfig.grid_size 10->11). Bias
    -> 0 across all matching exps; cue bias provably 0.
[x] Concatenated matching law -- AMOUNT term (exp011): identical VI, sweep amount
    ratio -> a_amt=0.97 (near-perfect), bias 0 after the geometry fix. Separable
    from rate sensitivity.
[x] Concatenated matching law -- PROBABILITY (exp013, a_p=0.78) and DELAY
    (exp014, a_d=0.61, hyperbolic/exponential selectable) terms. All four
    dimensions (rate/amount/probability/delay) reproduced with separable
    sensitivities, ~0 bias. Delay = reduced reinforcer EFFICACY (not credit-decay).

DECREMENTAL LEARNING / EXTINCTION (pluggable LearningRule)
---------------------------------------------------------
[x] Pluggable LearningRule interface (mirrors ConsequenceModel): RescorlaWagner
    + LinearOperator, selected by config.learning_model.
[x] RescorlaWagner: error-correcting w/ omission decay (lambda=0 on
    non-reinforced exposure, gated to contact) + asymmetric acq/ext rates.
    Acquisition latency 152->106; trained hw_food 1.0 extinguishes to ~0 over
    a few unreinforced lives (run_extinction_demo).
[ ] Dual excitatory/inhibitory (Konorski/Pearce-Hall/Bouton): separate w+ (grows)
    and w- (grows on omission, decays/context-gated) -> spontaneous recovery,
    renewal, rapid reacquisition.
[ ] Momentum-modulated decay (Nevin & Grace): extinction rate scaled by
    accumulated reinforcement / atom mass -> resistance to change, PREE.
[ ] Pearce-Hall variable associability (learning rate ~ |prediction error|).
[ ] Resurgence as Choice (Shahan & Craig, 2017, JEAB): resurgence via the
    matching/behavioral-momentum framework.
