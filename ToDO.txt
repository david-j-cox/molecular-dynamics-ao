[x] Subtractive (de Villiers, 1980): score = vr - c*vp (a punisher cancels c reinforcers).
    BOTH preps -- chamber (chamber.run_punishment_choice, allocation) AND foraging
    (consequence.Subtractive: aversive teaching signal scaled by c=punishment_weight so
